What is ColorBridge?

COLORBRIDGE is an AI-powered browser extension designed to make data visualization accessible for individuals with red-green color blindness. By automatically analyzing charts, graphs, and visual data on the web, it instantly converts them into high-contrast, easy-to-read formats. No more second-guessing color-coded metrics-COLORBRIDGE ensures you can interpret data accurately, effortlessly, and with total confidence.

What we offer

Everything the extension currently does, today.

👆

Click to analyse

Point at one chart, table or status label instead of scanning the whole page - faster, and it matches how the free plan counts usage.

🔒

Runs entirely on your machine

A vision-language model (Ollama + Qwen3-VL) runs locally. No cloud API, nothing about your screen ever leaves your computer.

🧠

Reads context, not assumptions

The legend, heading and caption around a chart decide what a colour means - the same green can mean "increase" on one page and "low risk" on another.

◭

Works on real chart types

Inline SVG charts, <canvas> charts (Chart.js and similar), raster images, and colour-coded HTML like status badges and tables.

↑↓

Shape-based symbols only

Arrows, checkmarks, crosses and warning signs - never a coloured dot. A symbol that only works for people who can already see colour is rejected automatically.

🚫

Knows when to do nothing

Photographs and decorative images are left untouched, and low-confidence guesses are refused rather than shown.

📄

PDF support

Chrome's built-in PDF viewer can't be reached the normal way, so PDFs use a dedicated flow: capture the page, drag a box around a chart, and get the answer right in the popup.
