AI Color Accessibility — Demo Page

A local vision model reads each graphic together with the text around it and works out what the colours mean here — it is never told that green is good.

  1. Start Ollama, and run npm start in backend/.
  2. Turn the extension ON, then press Select a chart to analyse.
  3. Click any graphic below. Analysis takes roughly 20 seconds on this machine.

A. Quarterly Financial Performance

Green candles indicate the stock price increased that day. Red candles indicate the price decreased that day.

Expected: green → increase (↑), red → decrease (↓).

B. Quiz Results

Correct answers are shown in green. Incorrect answers are shown in red.

  • Question 1: Correct
  • Question 2: Incorrect
  • Question 3: Correct
  • Question 4: Incorrect

Expected: green → correct (✓), red → incorrect (✕). Same colours as section A, different meaning.

C. Project Status Dashboard

Green means the task is completed, yellow means it is still pending, red means it failed.

D. Network Load by Hour

Green bars are hours of low network load. Red bars are hours of high load. High load is normal during business hours and is not a fault.

Expected: green → low load, red → high load — not "good" and "bad".

E. Project Risk Assessment

Risk level for this project. Green is low risk, yellow is medium risk, red is high risk.

Expected: symbols land exactly in the centre of each circle (SVG shapes are measured, not guessed).

F. Team Photo

Expected: nothing happens. Plenty of red and green, but none of it is data.

G. Overview

Expected: low confidence, so nothing is changed. The page never says what the colours stand for.
